Our founder

Alex Dalton founded John & Co. He is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran and the author of the memoir Warrior's Light: A Memoir of Faith, Fire & the American Soul. His story was featured by Anderson Magazine in “How One Anderson County Marine Turned Adversity into a Life of Purpose.”

John & Co. is how he intends to turn that commitment to service into durable, local work in agriculture and land services, built step by step and documented as it happens.

Frequently asked questions

What is John & Co.?

John & Co. Heritage Farms & Landholdings LLC is a veteran-led South Carolina limited liability company formed in October 2026. It is developing agricultural sourcing, production partnerships, and land-based services. These lines of work are planned and not yet operating.

Is John & Co. a certified SDVOSB, or registered in SAM.gov?

No certification or registration status is claimed. John & Co. does not claim SBA VetCert or SDVOSB certification, an active SAM.gov registration, or a CAGE code. This answer will change only when those records exist.

Does John & Co. own or farm land today?

No land ownership or control is claimed. Future production would begin only with documented site control, a viable use, willing partners, and a responsible operating plan.

Does John & Co. have contracts or customers?

No awarded contracts, customers, or past performance are claimed.

Is anything on this site an investment offer?

No. Nothing on this site is an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any security or investment. Plans and concepts are labeled and are not promises of returns.

How do I contact John & Co.?

Email daltonalex59@gmail.com. Include the customer, scope, location, timing, and volume. We reply by email.
