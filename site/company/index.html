Who we are

Our Company

Founded by a Marine from Anderson County, on a family standard of stewardship.

The founder

Johnathan Alexander “Alex” Dalton is a United States Marine Corps veteran, a Clemson University graduate, an author, and a veteran advocate. He grew up in Anderson County, South Carolina, where his family’s roots have long been tied to agriculture and the land.

Service

Alex enlisted in the Marine Corps, stepping onto the yellow footprints at Parris Island and going on to infantry training and Security Forces. He served in ceremonial and security duty in Washington, D.C., including at Camp David, and stood dignified transfer duty at Dover Air Force Base. He deployed to Helmand Province, Afghanistan, embedded with the Afghan 6th Kandak.

Education

After leaving the Corps he studied at Greenville Technical College and then Clemson University. On November 18, 2017, wearing #27, he carried the American flag down The Hill at Clemson. He earned his degree at Clemson University, a Bachelor of Science in Sociology, on May 8, 2020.

Roots and values

Some of his best early memories are of time on family land in Anderson County with his grandfather, whom the family called Papa. Papa measured a man by what he maintained: tools put back, a word kept, a place left better than he found it. That standard is the one John & Co. is built on.

The memoir

Alex is the author of Warrior's Light: A Memoir of Faith, Fire & the American Soul, which follows the road from Anderson County through Dover and Helmand Province to the family land he chose to steward. Read more at warriorslight.info.

His story was featured by Anderson Magazine as a “Person to Watch” in “How One Anderson County Marine Turned Adversity into a Life of Purpose” (July 3, 2026).

The company

John & Co. Heritage Farms & Landholdings LLC

A veteran-led limited liability company formed in South Carolina in October 2026 and based in Upstate South Carolina (Anderson County). John & Co. is developing three areas of work for buyers, producers, and landowners: agricultural sourcing and distribution, producer and processing relationships, and land and property services.
