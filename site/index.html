Who we are

A new company with old-fashioned standards.

John & Co. grows out of an Anderson County family’s long connection to farming and the land, and out of the discipline of the Marine Corps. Founder Alex Dalton grew up in Anderson County, served as a Marine in Washington, D.C., at Camp David, at Dover Air Force Base, and in Helmand Province, Afghanistan, then earned his degree at Clemson University.

The company was formed in South Carolina in October 2026. We are building it the way his grandfather kept the home place: tools put back, a word kept, and a place left better than we found it.

What we are building

Three areas of work, developed for real buyers and landowners.

Agricultural sourcing & distribution

For buyers who need a dependable, documented path to South Carolina agricultural goods—beef, poultry, hay and forage, and seasonal produce.

  • Start from your specification, volume, and delivery window
  • Match to qualified producers and processors
  • Traceability, inspection, and cold-chain checked before any commitment

Producer & processing relationships

For growers, livestock producers, and processors who want a straightforward partner to connect their capacity with real demand.

  • Clear terms built on actual capacity and rights
  • Fair, documented pricing
  • Records kept from the first load

Land & property services

For landowners who want land kept productive and cared for—scoped to the site, the season, and the owner’s goals.

  • Pasture, hay-field, and vegetation stewardship scopes
  • Property-care and forestry feasibility
  • Crew, equipment, and insurance confirmed per job

Each area is in development. We take on work only where the need, the capacity, and the paperwork are confirmed.

How we work

From a stated need to a delivered, documented job.

  1. 01

    Define the need

    Customer, scope, specifications, timing, destination, and acceptance criteria.

  2. 02

    Qualify the path

    Supplier capacity, site rights, licenses, insurance, food safety, and logistics.

  3. 03

    Price the whole job

    Product, labor, freight, storage, timing, and risk accounted for up front.

  4. 04

    Deliver and document

    Perform to the agreed standard and keep the records that prove it.
