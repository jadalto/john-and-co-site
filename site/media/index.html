Short bio for media

Johnathan Alexander “Alex” Dalton is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran, Clemson University graduate, and author of Warrior's Light: A Memoir of Faith, Fire & the American Soul. He founded John & Co. Heritage Farms & Landholdings LLC, a veteran-led company formed in South Carolina in October 2026 and based in Upstate South Carolina (Anderson County).
