Community and Veterans

Service shapes how this company is being built.

Documented

A veteran-led company

John & Co. was founded by Alex Dalton, a U.S. Marine Corps veteran. The discipline of service shapes how the company is being built: say what is true, plan the work, and follow through.

Planned · not yet operating

What we hope to build

  • Work in agriculture and land services that veterans can step into as the business grows.
  • Relationships with local producers, landowners, and buyers in the Upstate.
  • Honest, practical information for veterans who are interested in farming.

No hiring, training, or community program is operating today. We will describe one here only once it exists.

Resources for veterans and farmers
