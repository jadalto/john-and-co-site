Community and Veterans

Service shapes how this company is being built.

Built by a Marine, for the community that raised him

John & Co. was founded by Alex Dalton, a U.S. Marine Corps veteran from Anderson County. Before the company, his work after the Marine Corps included victim advocacy, mentoring, and community service. The same discipline shapes how this company is being built: say what is true, plan the work, and follow through.

“Leadership is responsibility.”Alex Dalton, quoted in Anderson Magazine

What we intend to build

  • Agriculture and land-service work that veterans can step into as the business grows
  • Lasting relationships with local producers, landowners, and buyers in the Upstate
  • Practical, honest information for veterans who are interested in farming

These are intentions; no hiring or training program is operating yet.
