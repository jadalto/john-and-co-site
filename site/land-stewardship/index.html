How capability would grow

Stage 1: services, sourcing, and resale

Build references, supplier discipline, customer trust, and positive unit economics.

Stage 2: cold chain, storage, and processing

Evaluate leased, contracted, or dedicated infrastructure only against verified throughput and offtake.

Stage 3: production and rural development

Pursue larger agricultural and community-development work after site rights, financing, testing, and paying demand are proven.
